## Overview

Cantor8 institutional look adapted for NNK: solid royal blue field, black product bands, white service band, floating pill nav, white CTA with arrow square, orange status dots, thin wireframe rectangles in the background. No water shader. No glass frost.

## Colors

`--blue #044ab3` owns most public surfaces. `--black #151515` for capability grids. White sections for pre-visit guidance. Emergency stays red. Orange dots label sections in Fragment Mono.

## Typography

Source Sans 3 at weight 400 for large headlines (Cantor8 uses light display weight). Fragment Mono for labels and phone rows.

## Components

Pill nav with white active chip. CTA = white rect + blue arrow cell. Cards are hairline borders on blue/black, not frosted glass.

## Do's and Don'ts

Do: oversized brand, asymmetrical hero, alternating blue/black/white bands, NNK facts only.
Don't: glassmorphism, water shader as identity, Athens-only services as NNK claims, health data in contact forms.
